## What Makes This Course Different

Most money education teaches you to **play by the bank's rules**: budget harder, save in the bank's account, get a better credit score so the bank will lend to you, and pay the bank's interest on everything you finance.

**We teach the other side of the table.** Every car, house, credit card and season you finance sends interest *out* of your family to a bank. Over a lifetime, that's often more money than families ever save. This course walks you, step by step, from *seeing* that leak, to *stopping* it, to *understanding how to capture it inside your own family banking system*.

We go in order, like a real training program. You don't learn the advanced play before the fundamentals:

| Phase | Weeks | Theme | You leave with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Pre-Season** | 1–4 | Face the numbers | Your ONE BILL, every debt and rate, your family's why |
| **2. Spring Training** | 5–8 | Take control of cash flow | The season off the card, a monthly plan, a starter cushion |
| **3. Regular Season** | 9–12 | How the bank really wins | Your lifetime interest number; protection in place |
| **4. Post-Season** | 13–16 | Become your own bank | A written family banking plan |

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the 16 weeks, every graduate will be able to:

1. State their family's **ONE BILL** (the full annual cost of youth sports) to the dollar.
2. List every debt the family carries, with its **interest rate** and **total yearly interest**.
3. Pay for the season with a **steady monthly amount**, with **no new sports charges on credit cards**.
4. Explain, in plain words, **how banks make money from a family**, and calculate their own lifetime interest.
5. Name their family's **protection gaps** (if the earner couldn't work, or died).
6. Explain the **strengths and limits** of dividend-paying whole life as a family banking tool, including guaranteed vs. non-guaranteed values, loans, interest and the years it takes to build.
7. Hold a **written 12-month and 5-year family banking plan**.
8. **Teach one other family** one idea from the course.

## Team Rules (Course Expectations)

**1. Show up.** Attend live, or watch the replay within 72 hours. Tell your Bill Buddy if you'll miss.

**2. Do the reps.** Each week's assignment is due **before** the next session. Late work is accepted once per season. After that, your coach will call you, because we don't let teammates fall behind.

**3. Check in with your Bill Buddy.** One five-minute check-in every week with your assigned accountability partner.

**4. Bring the real numbers.** Estimates are fine in Week 1. By Week 3, we work with real statements.

**5. No shame, no judgment.** Every family starts somewhere. We celebrate progress, not perfection.

**6. What's said in the locker room stays in the locker room.** Never share another family's numbers or story outside the group.

**7. No selling in the gym.** Nobody (members, guests or staff) pitches products in group sessions. Questions about a specific policy or your personal situation go to a one-on-one with a licensed professional, only if you ask for it.

**8. Both partners in, when possible.** If you share finances with someone, they're welcome at every session and expected at Weeks 4 and 15.

## The Season Schedule

### PHASE 1: PRE-SEASON · Face the Numbers

**Week 1: Orientation · The Three Kinds of Broke**
- *Objective:* Understand that broke is financial, physical and mental, and a matter of awareness, and commit to the season.
- *Reading:* Book, "What Does Broke Actually Mean?" and "The Real Starting Point"
- *In session:* Team Rules, Bill Buddy assignments, the Gatorade-and-cooler lesson
- *Assignment due Week 2:* Take the Sports Money DNA survey. Track **every dollar** for 7 days.

**Week 2: Your ONE BILL**
- *Objective:* Turn the whole season into one number.
- *Reading:* The Season Budget, Step 1 · Book, Reason 1
- *In session:* Build each family's ONE BILL: club fees, travel, hotels, food, gear, lessons.
- *Assignment due Week 3:* Your ONE BILL for the year, written on the Stat Sheet.

**Week 3: Who's Banking on You?**
- *Objective:* See every debt, every rate, and how much interest leaves your family each year.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 4
- *In session:* The Debt & Interest Sheet: balance, rate, minimum payment and yearly interest for every card, loan, car and mortgage.
- *Assignment due Week 4:* A complete Debt & Interest Sheet using real statements, plus the family's **total yearly interest** number.

**Week 4: The Family Why · Pre-Season Physical** ✅ *Checkpoint 1*
- *Objective:* Agree as a household on what you're building and the rules you'll play by.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 11
- *In session:* Partners attend. Write the family why and the Family Game Plan (spending rules, who pays what, how decisions get made).
- *Assignment due Week 5:* A signed Family Game Plan. **Checkpoint 1:** ONE BILL, Debt & Interest Sheet and Game Plan all turned in.

### PHASE 2: SPRING TRAINING · Take Control of Cash Flow

**Week 5: Chosen or Drifted?**
- *Objective:* Find the money that's leaking without a decision.
- *Reading:* Book, Reasons 2 and 3
- *In session:* Mark every recurring expense Chosen or Drifted. Build the Tournament Weekend Playbook (cooler, meal plan, hotel rules).
- *Assignment due Week 6:* Cut or renegotiate at least **two** drifted expenses. Report the monthly savings.

**Week 6: Divide by 12**
- *Objective:* Stop letting the club's billing calendar run your family.
- *Reading:* The Season Budget, "The Biggest Win Needs No Policy"
- *In session:* ONE BILL ÷ 12 = your monthly season payment, including October through December.
- *Assignment due Week 7:* Open a dedicated Season Fund and set up the **automatic monthly transfer**. Send a screenshot to your Bill Buddy.

**Week 7: Get Off the Card**
- *Objective:* Stop paying interest to play.
- *Reading:* The Season Budget, "Swipe the Card" vs. "Save, Then Pay Cash"
- *In session:* $6,900 on a 22.9% card costs $354 in interest and still leaves $268 owed in January. Build each family's payoff order.
- *Assignment due Week 8:* **No new sports charges on credit cards** from today. A written payoff plan for any existing sports debt.

**Week 8: The Emergency Bench · Mid-Season Review** ✅ *Checkpoint 2*
- *Objective:* Build the cushion that keeps one bad month from becoming new debt.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 9
- *In session:* Starter cushion targets; where to keep it.
- *Assignment due Week 9:* Start the cushion (any amount, automated). **Checkpoint 2:** a 15-minute one-on-one Stat Sheet review with your coach.

### PHASE 3: REGULAR SEASON · How the Bank Really Wins

**Week 9: How the Bank Makes Money on Your Family**
- *Objective:* Understand interest, amortization and why the early years of a loan are mostly interest.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 7
- *In session:* Read your own mortgage and car loan amortization schedules. Where does each payment really go?
- *Assignment due Week 10:* Your **lifetime interest number**: total interest scheduled on every loan you have.

**Week 10: You Finance Everything You Buy**
- *Objective:* See that paying cash also has a cost: money spent from savings stops earning.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 10
- *In session:* Borrowing costs interest. Paying cash costs what that money would have earned. Either way, the money leaves. The question is whose system it flows through.
- *Assignment due Week 11:* List the family's next three big purchases (car, season, home repair) and how each would normally be paid for.

**Week 11: Protect the Player**
- *Objective:* Protection comes before strategy.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 5
- *In session:* What happens to the family if the earner can't work for six months, or dies? Life, disability and accident coverage explained in plain language.
- *Assignment due Week 12:* Write down your current coverage. Optional: ask a licensed professional the six-month question.

**Week 12: The 5-Tool Player**
- *Objective:* Understand dividend-paying whole life honestly, strengths and weaknesses both.
- *Reading:* **The 5-Tool Player** · Book, Reason 6
- *In session:* The five tools; the "farm system" (early years cost more than they're worth); the guaranteed and non-guaranteed columns; how it compares to a savings account, CD and 401(k).
- *Assignment due Week 13:* Answer the five review questions on the handout in your own words.

### PHASE 4: POST-SEASON · Become Your Own Bank

**Week 13: How the Money Moves**
- *Objective:* Learn the borrow, pay and repay cycle, and why the same dollar never pays two bills.
- *Reading:* **How the Money Moves**
- *In session:* The three money streams (premium, loan payments, club fees); the six-step cycle; the October–December reset; where it falls short when payments are skipped.
- *Assignment due Week 14:* Map your own season through the six steps using your ONE BILL.

**Week 14: Recapture**
- *Objective:* Understand how families redirect the interest and principal they send to banks back through a system they own, and what it honestly takes.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 8
- *In session:* Paying yourself back with the same discipline you'd pay a bank. The real requirements: years of funding, loan interest, repayment discipline, and the risks if loans go unpaid. Compare: $354 to a card company vs. $86 of policy loan interest (from The Season Budget example).
- *Assignment due Week 15:* A draft of your family banking plan: which bills come first, and what you'd fund and when.

**Week 15: Your Family Banking Plan**
- *Objective:* Put it all in writing.
- *Reading:* Book, "The Final Score"
- *In session:* Partners attend. Finalize the 12-month and 5-year plan. Families who want to explore a policy may **request** a one-on-one with a licensed professional. It is never required to graduate.
- *Assignment due Week 16:* A final written family banking plan. Re-take the Sports Money DNA survey.

**Week 16: Championship Night** ✅ *Checkpoint 3*
- *Objective:* Celebrate, measure and pass it on.
- *Reading:* Book, Reason 12
- *In session:* Before-and-after Stat Sheets, the community's total Bills Killed, graduate stories, certificates, and the Champions Wall.
- *Final assignment:* Teach one idea to one family outside the course within 30 days.
