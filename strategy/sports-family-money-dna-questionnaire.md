# The Sports Family Money DNA Questionnaire

*For Ball Without Boundaries. Sports parents only. Draft v1, November 7, 2026.*

**Two parts:**
- **Part A, the Quick DNA:** 12 questions, about 4 minutes. Everyone takes it. Gives each family their "Money DNA" type.
- **Part B, the Deep Dive:** optional, about 8 more minutes. For founding members and anyone who wants a personal follow-up.

---

## Intro Screen (copy)

> **How does your family *really* pay for the game?**
>
> Every report says youth sports costs keep climbing, and that a lot of families "go into debt." But nobody has asked *how*. Credit cards? Home equity? A second job? Grandparents?
>
> Ball Without Boundaries is finding out, so we can help sports families keep their kids playing **without wrecking everything else**.
>
> - Takes about 4 minutes
> - **Anonymous** if you want it to be
> - We **never sell your data**. Results are only shared as group totals.
>
> At the end, you'll get your family's **Sports Money DNA** type.

## Results Screen: The Sports Money DNA Types

Score each family from **Question 6 (main payment method)**, adjusted by **Q7 (debt)** and **Q8 (trade-offs)**. Each type gets a short, non-judgmental description and its ONE BILL next step.

| DNA type | Main payment method | What it means | ONE BILL next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **The Planner** | Sports savings / sinking fund | You're ahead of the game. | Help other families; optimize costs (travel hacks, bulk gear). |
| 🔵 **The Cash-Flow Grinder** | Regular paycheck | You're making it work, but any surprise breaks the budget. | Build a "Season Fund," the first ONE BILL. |
| 🟡 **The Card Swiper** | Credit card paid in full | Smart on points, but spending can drift. | Set a season cap; use rewards for travel. |
| 🟠 **The Balance Carrier** | Credit card with a balance / BNPL / club payment plans | Sports is quietly costing you **interest on top of fees**. | **Your ONE bill: the card.** Payoff plan, rate negotiation, balance transfer education. |
| 🔴 **The Equity Tapper** | HELOC / cash-out refi / personal loan | Your **house** is paying for the game. | Know your rate and payoff timeline; build a plan so next season isn't borrowed. |
| 🟣 **The Future Borrower** | 401(k) / retirement / cut retirement contributions | Today's game is paid by your **future self**. | Protect retirement first; find scholarships and sponsorships. |
| ⚪ **The Village-Funded** | Grandparents / family / fundraising / second job | It takes a village, and you've got one. | Make it sustainable; plan for when help changes. |

## How to Use the Data

1. **Inside BWB:** tag each member with their DNA type and route them to the right challenge or space.
2. **Monthly:** share anonymous, aggregate stats as community posts ("38% of us carry a sports card balance").
3. **Twice a year:** publish **"The Real Cost of the Game"** report. This is your PR engine: original data on *how* families pay, which nobody else publishes.
4. **Into ONE BILL:** every DNA type has a clear "your ONE bill" next step, which is the bridge to the ONE BILL community.

## Build Notes

- Build it in a form tool with branching logic so Part B only shows if they opt in. Circle forms, Jotform or Typeform all work; embed it in the BWB community and share a public link too.
- Keep Part A on phone-friendly single screens with a progress bar.
- Ask for an email only at the end, and keep it optional.
- **Q5 is the money question.** Never shorten it. It's the data nobody else has.
